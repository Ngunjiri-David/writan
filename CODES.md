# CODES.md

## Client of one
Built on [you], fitted on [your partner].
For [you], this should feel like setting a heavy bag down.
I will know because you capture in under three seconds, settle Today in
under a minute each morning, and by week two keep no list elsewhere.
Second proof: in week two your partner is still using it unprompted.

## Codes
- Voice: plain and brief. Concrete verbs, sentences a person would say aloud.
  No praise, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Material: warm paper and ink. One open-licence type family with real weights
  and italics and tabular figures, chosen at the toile and for a reason.
- Proportion and density: 8 dp unit, wide outer margins, one task per line,
  rows never under 48 dp.
- Tempo and motion: motion explains cause and effect, 150-300 ms, easing out.
  Completing a task draws one line through it, the row rests a beat, then
  leaves. One soft haptic on completion, nowhere else. Reduced motion: instant.
- Color: tinted neutral ramp (warm paper by day, warm graphite by night), one
  accent used for one thing per screen, body contrast 4.5:1 or better.
  Dynamic color off: the house has a hand.
- Silence: no popups, badges, streaks, tips, rating prompts or upsells.
  Notifications only for reminders the owner set.
- Ownership: data lives on the device. No account. Export to a plain file.

## Never
- Exclamation marks, emoji, stock photography, confetti, streaks, scores
- Red "overdue" on anything that has no deadline
- A first-run tour or modal
- Anything copied from Things (icon, wording, layout, animation), or its name
  in the app or the Play listing

## References (and what we take from each)
- A hand-finished watch dial (Dufour): one face, only what is needed to read it
- Swiss railway clock: legible at a glance, a single red accent used once
- A bound ledger with entries struck through: completion as a drawn line
- A tea room (Kenya Hara's emptiness): an empty Today is a finished state
  with its own quiet page, never a failure
- The Rolls-Royce door umbrella: one thing found, never announced

## The hidden delight
Under the oldest entry in the Ledger, one line: "Begun" and the first date.

## Decisions
- Structure borrowed, surface original
- Local-first, no sync. Each user keeps their own list (confirmed, for now).
  Completed list is called Ledger (default).
- Candidate for the spec: carry-over as a question asked once each morning
  (keep for today / move to Upcoming / let go to Someday), never a red pile
- Open: app name, typeface, accent, stack
